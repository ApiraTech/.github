# Security Policy

## Supported versions

Unless a repository states otherwise, security updates target its default branch and latest
documented release. Older releases may no longer receive security updates.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email [tondrey@wovensolutions.com](mailto:tondrey@wovensolutions.com). Do not open a public issue
or discussion for a suspected vulnerability.

Please include:

- the affected repository, version, or commit;
- a description of the issue and its potential impact;
- steps to reproduce or a minimal proof of concept; and
- any known mitigations or workarounds.

Do not include credentials, personal data, or other third-party secrets. We will acknowledge the
report and coordinate validation, remediation, and disclosure with you.

## Safe research

Act in good faith, avoid privacy violations and service disruption, and test only systems and
data you are authorized to use. Allow a reasonable remediation period before public disclosure.
