# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free community for everyone,
regardless of identity, background, experience, or level of participation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Welcome questions and differing viewpoints.
- Focus criticism on ideas and work, not people.
- Respect privacy and the boundaries of others.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, sustained
disruption, and publishing another person's private information are unacceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in project spaces and when someone publicly represents an ApiraTech project.
Repository maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently limit
participation when behavior violates this code.

Report abusive GitHub content through GitHub's reporting tools and contact the relevant repository
maintainers privately when possible. Reports will be handled as confidentially as circumstances
allow. Retaliation against a reporter is itself a violation.
